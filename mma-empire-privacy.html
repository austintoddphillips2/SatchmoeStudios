MMA Empire Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Satchmoe Studios provides MMA Empire. This policy explains how information is handled when you play on iPhone, iPad, Windows, macOS, or in a browser, and when you contact us. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to satchmoestudios@gmail.com.

Your game data

MMA Empire does not require a game account. Careers, names you enter, game progress, play time, achievements, settings, and tutorial preferences are stored on your device. Native apps use local application storage, including recovery copies; browser versions use browser storage. Satchmoe Studios does not automatically receive your career files or operate a cloud-save service for this game.

Imports and exports happen when you choose them. JSON and ZIP exports may contain your career names and other information you entered. An exported file is saved to the destination you select. If that destination is a cloud drive, or you share the file, that service or recipient receives the file under their own terms. Your operating system or store platform may also include app data in backups according to your settings.

Advertising on iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage advertising consent where required. Ads may be personalized using information processed by Google and its advertising partners, depending on your consent choices, device permissions, and applicable requirements. Where personalization is not permitted, Google may serve non-personalized or limited ads, or no ads. Non-personalized ads can still involve data collection.

Google's advertising software may receive your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device or app identifiers, crash and performance information, and information about ads and app interactions. These are used to deliver and measure ads, personalize ads where permitted, operate and improve the advertising service, and detect abuse. Where permitted, personalization may use your activity across other apps and websites and interests inferred by Google and its advertising partners. The information processed depends on your consent, device permissions, and Google's configuration.

You can review available consent choices under Settings → Data → Ad Privacy → Privacy Options. Choices may vary by region. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google’s consent messages explain the purposes and advertising partners involved and request consent where required, including for personalization. You can revisit available choices and withdraw consent through Privacy Options. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal. Apple’s tracking permission is separate from regional advertising consent; the app requests non-personalized ads when Apple tracking permission is absent. You can change tracking permission in your device’s privacy settings. Refusing consent or tracking does not prevent you from playing the game. Where Remove Ads is offered, an active purchase disables in-game ads; it does not erase information already processed by an advertising provider. The Windows, macOS, and browser versions do not use the game's iOS AdMob integration.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosures for its practices and controls.

Purchases and store services

Apple processes purchases and restores made through the App Store. The app checks purchase entitlement information to enable purchased features. Satchmoe Studios does not receive your payment-card details through the game. Apple and other platforms from which you obtain the game handle their own account, payment, and platform information under their policies. See Apple's Privacy Policy and, if you use Steam, Steam's Privacy Policy.

Support and ad reports

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests. An ad report may include the game version, report time, details you enter, and available details about the last ad, such as its time, ad unit, response identifier, network, and whether it was a test ad.

The ad-report form prepares a message for you to review and send in your email app. It does not automatically send a report or attach a career file. The app keeps the last displayed ad's context and the ad timing counter locally to support reporting and scheduling.

Online content and external links

Some game screens load images from Unsplash. Loading an online image can disclose network information, such as your IP address and request details, to the image provider. See Unsplash's Privacy Policy. Links to Discord, Steam, our website, or other external services open those services, whose privacy policies apply. The public policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives network request information when you visit it; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Sharing and protection

We do not automatically upload your careers for advertising or analytics, and the game has no separate Satchmoe Studios analytics or crash-reporting service. The third-party processing described above supports advertising, purchases, online content, hosting, and email. Providers process information under their privacy and data-protection terms. Providers acting on our behalf must protect personal information consistently with this policy and applicable law. We may disclose information you send us when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or prevent abuse.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local game information remains until it is replaced or removed. You can export careers and delete active careers from the save-selection screen. Recovery or migration copies, settings, and lifetime statistics may remain separately. Resetting settings does not erase careers. To remove all local information, remove the app's stored data; on iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes its local container, while offloading the app retains data. Desktop uninstallers may retain application data. Contact us if you need help finding and removing it.

Exported files and device or cloud backups must be removed separately through the locations or services holding them. We cannot remotely erase files on your device or copies you have shared.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve requests, maintain relevant support records, and meet legal obligations. You can email us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have sent us. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. We will handle applicable requests and explain any legal reason information must be retained.

Google, Apple, other platforms, and email or hosting providers control retention of information they process. Their privacy policies explain their retention and deletion options. Information may be processed in countries other than your own through these services.

General audience

MMA Empire is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed to children. We do not ask players to provide their age, gender, or contact details to play. If you believe a child has sent us personal information that should be removed, contact us at the email below.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The effective date identifies the current version. Material changes will be explained as appropriate through the game or our website.
