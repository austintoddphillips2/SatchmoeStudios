Satchmoe Studios Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Satchmoe Studios provides MMA Empire and this website. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit our website, play MMA Empire on iPhone, iPad, Windows, macOS, or in a browser, or contact us. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to satchmoestudios@gmail.com.

Your game data

MMA Empire does not require a game account. Careers, names you enter, game progress, play time, achievements, settings, and tutorial preferences are stored on your device. Native apps use local application storage, including recovery copies; browser versions use browser storage. Satchmoe Studios does not automatically receive your career files or operate a cloud-save service for this game.

Imports and exports happen when you choose them. JSON and ZIP exports may contain your career names and other information you entered. An exported file is saved to the destination you select. If that destination is a cloud drive, or you share the file, that service or recipient receives the file under their own terms. Your operating system or store platform may also include app data in backups according to your settings.

Advertising on iPhone and iPad

The iPhone and iPad app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to display ads and manage advertising consent where required. Ads may be personalized using information processed by Google and its advertising partners, depending on your consent choices, device permissions, and applicable requirements. Where personalization is not permitted, Google may serve non-personalized or limited ads, or no ads. Non-personalized ads can still involve data collection.

Google's advertising software may receive your IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device or app identifiers, crash and performance information, and information about ads and app interactions. These are used to deliver and measure ads, personalize ads where permitted, operate and improve the advertising service, and detect abuse. Where permitted, personalization may use your activity across other apps and websites and interests inferred by Google and its advertising partners. The information processed depends on your consent, device permissions, and Google's configuration.

You can review available consent choices under Settings → Data → Ad Privacy → Privacy Options. Choices may vary by region. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google’s consent messages explain the purposes and advertising partners involved and request consent where required, including for personalization. You can revisit available choices and withdraw consent through Privacy Options. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before withdrawal. Apple’s tracking permission is separate from regional advertising consent; the app requests non-personalized ads when Apple tracking permission is absent. You can change tracking permission in your device’s privacy settings. Refusing consent or tracking does not prevent you from playing the game. Where Remove Ads is offered, an active purchase disables in-game ads; it does not erase information already processed by an advertising provider. The Windows, macOS, and browser versions do not use the game's iOS AdMob integration.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosures for its practices and controls.

Purchases and store services

Apple processes purchases and restores made through the App Store. The app checks purchase entitlement information to enable purchased features. Satchmoe Studios does not receive your payment-card details through the game. Apple and other platforms from which you obtain the game handle their own account, payment, and platform information under their policies. See Apple's Privacy Policy and, if you use Steam, Steam's Privacy Policy.

Support and ad reports

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests. An ad report may include the game version, report time, details you enter, and available details about the last ad, such as its time, ad unit, response identifier, network, and whether it was a test ad.

The ad-report form prepares a message for you to review and send in your email app. It does not automatically send a report or attach a career file. The app keeps the last displayed ad's context and the ad timing counter locally to support reporting and scheduling.

External links

Links to Discord, Steam, our website, or other external services open those services, whose privacy policies apply. The public policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives network request information when you visit it; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Website analytics

Our Satchmoe Studios website uses Google Analytics to understand visits and how people use its pages. Google Analytics may collect page views and other configured website interactions, browser and device information, approximate location, and identifiers stored in first-party cookies. Google processes this information for website measurement. The website analytics tag is separate from MMA Empire's game code and does not upload your locally stored careers or documents. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Optional gameplay analytics

On iPhone and iPad, you can choose to share gameplay analytics under Settings → Data → Share gameplay analytics. Sharing is off by default. Enabling sharing allows the collection and upload of the gameplay summaries described below; it does not enable personalized advertising or change your advertising privacy choices. You can play and use purchased features without enabling sharing, and a Remove Ads purchase does not enable or disable this separate choice. We use these reports to improve gameplay, performance, and reliability, not for advertising.

Reports contain random session references, a random analytics profile reference that connects visits on this installation, visit start times and return intervals, app-open and cold-launch counts, career-session counts, app version and build, operating-system major version, phone or tablet category and hardware model, and numeric summaries of active playtime, simulation duration and frequency, weeks advanced, completed events, roster additions, new careers created, ad loading and display, tutorial progress, and save successes or failures. Simulation context includes career year, roster-size group, and whether it was an event week. We keep separate summaries of the first three observed app visits and cumulative totals, including playtime across careers, to understand return visits and engagement. This history begins when you enable sharing. Reports do not include career files, names you enter, messages, email addresses, advertising identifiers, or hardware device identifiers. A hardware model identifies a device type shared by many people, not your individual device.

An app visit can include opening only the main menu. Career sessions are measured separately. Active playtime includes time in foreground career screens and menus, without idle detection; it excludes background time and recognized ad, advertising-consent, and purchase or restore interruptions. Simulation measurements describe the processing operation and do not include the full wait for every animation or fight presentation. Reports can be incomplete when the app is force-closed or an upload is unavailable. Earlier app use and historical save-file playtime are not added when you first enable sharing.

Reports are sent to Satchmoe Studios through Apple's CloudKit service when an iCloud account and a connection are available. This analytics integration does not use Firebase or a separate third-party analytics service. Apple processes these records and adds system metadata, including a creator reference associated with the iCloud account. These reports are therefore not described as fully anonymous. The app keeps an account reference locally to help avoid sending queued reports under a different account. Access to reports is restricted through CloudKit permissions. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Unsent reports are stored separately from career saves on your device and excluded from app backups. The queue is limited to 200 records. Reports become ineligible for upload after seven days and are discarded during subsequent queue maintenance while the app runs. The local analytics profile and cumulative totals remain between visits; recent visit and session history is bounded, and the first-three-visit summaries are retained. This history is reset when sharing is turned off, the app's local data is deleted, or the app detects an iCloud account change.

Turning sharing off stops collection, discards the current report, removes the local analytics profile, history and unsent reports, and requests cancellation of any upload in progress. Enabling sharing again starts a new random profile and history. An upload already accepted by Apple may complete. Turning sharing off does not automatically erase previously uploaded reports. Uploaded reports are retained for development and reliability analysis until removed through our data maintenance or a deletion request; the app does not automatically delete them from CloudKit when you disable sharing. Contact us at the address below to request deletion or discuss your privacy rights. If additional information is needed to locate your reports, we will explain what is needed.

Sharing and protection

We do not upload your career files for advertising or analytics. Optional gameplay summaries are described above; there is no separate automatic crash-reporting service operated by Satchmoe Studios. The third-party processing described in this policy supports advertising, purchases, optional gameplay analytics, website analytics, hosting, and email. Providers process information under their privacy and data-protection terms. Providers acting on our behalf must protect personal information consistently with this policy and applicable law. We may disclose information you send us when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or prevent abuse.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local game information remains until it is replaced or removed. You can export careers and delete active careers from the save-selection screen. Recovery or migration copies, settings, and lifetime statistics may remain separately. Resetting settings does not erase careers. To remove all local information, remove the app's stored data; on iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes its local container, while offloading the app retains data. Desktop uninstallers may retain application data. Contact us if you need help finding and removing it.

Exported files and device or cloud backups must be removed separately through the locations or services holding them. We cannot remotely erase files on your device or copies you have shared.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve requests, maintain relevant support records, and meet legal obligations. You can email us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have sent us. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, request portability, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority. We will handle applicable requests and explain any legal reason information must be retained.

Google, Apple, other platforms, and email or hosting providers control retention of information they process. Their privacy policies explain their retention and deletion options. Information may be processed in countries other than your own through these services.

General audience

MMA Empire is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed to children. We do not ask players to provide their age, gender, or contact details to play. If you believe a child has sent us personal information that should be removed, contact us at the email below.
